# The Acton Network: How Academies Are Organized

How the Acton Academy network works around a single academy: who decides, what
software academies already use, and where owners and Guides talk to each other.
Read this before writing anything that assumes how an academy adopts a tool.

Items marked *(from the Eagle Bot author)* come from people inside the network.
Don't overwrite them with public research just because the public web doesn't
mention them. Many of these details are internal to the network's owner portal.

## Structure

**Zones** *(from the Eagle Bot author)*
Academies are sorted into zones. Public sources don't describe them. The
network's owner portal (network.actonacademy.org) requires a login. Ask an
owner how zones work (who leads them, whether they meet, how often) before
building plans around them.

**IT department** *(from the Eagle Bot author)*
The network has an IT department. Public sources don't say what it does for
academies or whether it vets tools.

**Owners decide for their own academy** *(from the Eagle Bot author; partly
confirmed publicly)*
Specific people at an academy (usually the owner) can adopt a tool on their own
authority, with no sign-off from the network. The network describes itself as
decentralized and says owners have "a tremendous amount of freedom" over
tuition, branding, enrollment tools and marketing
([source](https://start.actonacademy.org/join-the-acton-academy-tribe)). Owners
also agree to uphold Acton's mission. A tool that works against learner
ownership would get pushback even though nobody has to approve it.

## Software academies already use

**Journey Tracker**
Most academies use it. It is the network's own software (developer "Acton
Academy LLC") and holds the learning design. It tracks goal setting and points,
Quest progress, Individual Badge Plans, Hero/Eagle Bucks, 360 peer reviews and
weekly reflections, and parents can see their child's work and badge progress.
Academies pay about $50/month in year one, then $100/month. It has a web app
and, since October 2025, iOS and Android apps.
([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/journey-tracker-by-acton/id6753354988),
[network page](https://start.actonacademy.org/join-the-acton-academy-tribe))

As far as public information shows, it has **no public API**, and it does not
cover Contracts, Town Halls, elections or positions. That gap is where Eagle Bot
fits. Eagle Bot should sit next to Journey Tracker, not compete with it.

**Journey Suite**
A separate operations platform "made by Acton people": marketing, enrollment and
parent community. It integrates with Journey Tracker and costs $179–$2,000 a
month. ([journey-suite.com](https://journey-suite.com/))

**Core Skills software**
Khan Academy and similar adaptive programs for math and reading are common.

## Screens and AI

- No academy is known to have a rigid no-screens policy for the whole school.
- Some keep the **Spark** studio (the youngest learners) screen-free on purpose,
  e.g. [Acton Columbus](https://actonacademycolumbus.com/montessori-reggio-emilia-preschool-kindergarten/).
  Older studios use software for Core Skills.
- No academy was found with a formal "no AI" policy, and the network has no
  published AI policy. Some academies write openly about AI, e.g.
  [Acton NW Indianapolis](https://actonindy.org/still-smarter-chatgpt/).

## Where owners and Guides are

- **LinkedIn and other social media.** Many owners and Guides are on LinkedIn,
  and it's a realistic way to reach them directly *(from the Eagle Bot
  author)*. The network has an official
  [LinkedIn page](https://www.linkedin.com/company/acton-academy) and
  [Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/ActonAcademy/), and most academies
  run their own Facebook page.
- **Owner's and Guide's Forums.** Private forums inside the network. Owners
  already offer paid tools to each other there. Getting in takes an owner.
- **Owner's Conference.** Held yearly in Austin, in person, with 160+ attendees
  in 2025.
- **Podcasts.** Jeff and Laura Sandefer appear on education podcasts (e.g.
  Getting Smart).
- No public virtual owner calls or vendor slots were found.

## What this means for Eagle Bot

- Eagle Bot is for **learners** to organize their own rules. No academy relies on
  Guides or owners to remember the Contract, positions and past decisions. The
  learners own them, and that is the Acton philosophy.
- Eagle Bucks and other economies aren't part of Eagle Bot. Journey Tracker
  already covers them, and an academy doesn't need to run one to want Eagle Bot.
