# **Hero Bucks**

 *A system to protect your boundaries and keep fellow travelers accountable*

It’s never nice when someone takes advantage of you. Hero Bucks give you a tool to protect your rights and enforce your boundaries. When someone violates a promise to the group, Hero Bucks provide a way to hold them accountable with a consequence.

1. **Earn Hero Bucks**   
   Exchange points earned during Core Skills work, Quest work, and for serving the community:  
   * Document work on Journey Tracker and report to the Hero Buck Committee.   
   * Must be earned — may not be borrowed or loaned.  
   * ***100 points \= 1 Hero Buck***

2. **Ask for a Hero Buck**   
   When someone breaks a promise or crosses a line:  
   * Say: *“We/you agreed \_\_\_ and you violated that by \_\_\_ so I am asking you for a Hero Buck.”*  
   * Go to the Hero Buck table.   
   * The person who was asked for a Hero Buck fills out the slip with the reason the Hero Buck was requested and checks “agree” or “disagree” to acknowledge their acceptance of this consequence (or not).   
   * Both Heroes sign the slip.

3. **Appeal a lost Hero Buck**   
   If you believe you are asked unjustly, launch the Appeals process:  
   * Both Heroes will be called to meet and tell their sides of the story to the Hero Buck Committee.   
   * The Committee decides if the Hero gets the Buck back. 

4. Form a Hero Buck Committee through Council, an independent vote, or however your studio sees fit. 

## **Hero Buck Committee**

Many studios will have a council or committee who will oversee Hero Bucks that is elected newly every session and is responsible for:

* Managing the Hero Buck Bank – on Fridays, give new Hero Bucks to those who earn them and collect those that have been taken away.  
* Changes to the Hero Buck system by bringing new items to Town Meeting for a vote  
* Hearing and making decisions on appeals.  Generally it is a good idea for each level of appeal to have an exponentially increasing cost to discourage unnecessary appeals.  
* Journey Tracker has tools to make gathering Hero Bucks and tracking the health of a studio easier.
