# Accountibility Partners Tac-On

I need you to build a Tac-On designed to aid the currently shaky AP (acccountibily partner) system. Refer to Acton_Context folder if unsure of the functionality and use of this system.

### The Problem

At the moment, many academies use the AP system. One of the incentives to be a good AP used by many Actons is the idea that if your AP does not reach their goal or fall behind, they don't get the reward they would have recieved otherwise, and neither do you, even if you did meet your goal. Obviosuly, there are exceptions, to this, but this is genrally the rule of thumb. The issue is that it is very diffuclt to keep a good record of how often you check in with your AP, and see what evidence was shown to prove that the AP had been working. 

### Our Solution

This Tac-On should start off with a screen in which the admin who downloaded it will be presented with two adacent colums titled AP 1 and AP 2. There will then be a list of dropwdowns, in which the Admin can select any of the learners to be any one of an AP pair. The same will be true for the other column. Also, add support for a the admin being able to create groups of three. Note that an admin or guide should be able to alter these parirings at any time.

> *Admins should be able to control wether or not they can assign themselves.*

Once the pairs are assigned, the accounts of the assigned APs are then coupled and each givin acess to their pair specifc dashboard of sorts.

Every user will have a dashboard with two tbas titled "You" and "[Your APs name]." Each of these dashbaords should allow for APs to check in with one another in a system where the AP can specify their goal for the weak, and the progress they'd made to that goal at the time of the check in, which must meet the follwoing requirments: 
APs are strongly encouraged to check on their counterparts every day of the week. APs are required to check in on their counterparts at least 3 days a week, one of which must be Friday. The tac-on should keep a calendar of when the check ins occured and how many more are left. Think about this from the perspective of a busy learner, and what would be helpful.

The above information is entirily, for core goals (eg, math and reading). During AP check ins, writers-workshop, civilizatian and quest should also be checked. We will use a system of uploading screen-shots with a field for notes on how much is done and why some of it might not be. As well as a dropdown to select wether or not the AP is on track for the week, with an additonal notes field as well. 

I can't describe this project in full, and what I have written above is simply the genral idea, so I need you to get creative and think about this logically. Also, feel free to make the changes to the Tac-On engine you need to make this project sucessful.
