# 01 · Audience

Every Acton academy is its own small business. There is no district, no
purchasing office, and no IT department. One or two people decide, and they
decide quickly if they see it working.

## Who's involved in a sale

| Role | What they care about | What they need from us | Their part in the sale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Enrollment, reputation with parents, not drowning in admin, staying true to Acton | Proof it saves Guides time and doesn't undercut learner ownership | **Buyer.** Signs up and pays. |
| **Guide** | Less paperwork, fewer "what does the Contract actually say?" arguments, calmer Town Halls | Something learners can run without them | **Champion or blocker.** If a Guide hates it, the pilot dies. |
| **Learner (Hero / Eagle)** | Owning their studio, real responsibility, building things people use | A tool that's theirs, not the adults' | **Daily user and best salesperson.** |
| **Parent** | Safety, privacy, "is my kid learning?" | A clear answer about data and AI | **Veto risk.** Rarely involved, but one worried parent can stop a rollout. |

## Who to sell to first

Not every academy is a good first customer. Start with the ones most likely to
say yes and most likely to tell other owners.

**Best early customers:**

- Have **Middle Studio or Launchpad** running. Older learners run Town Halls
  well, and they're the ones who'll build Tac-Ons.
- Have a **written Contract and ROE** already (Google Docs count). The
  Contract → Wiki scan needs something to read.
- Already run **Hero / Eagle Bucks** and **elected positions**. Eagle Bot
  handles both, so the value is obvious from day one.
- Have an owner who **posts in owner forums** or shows up at gatherings. They'll
  talk about it.
- Have **2+ studios**. More studios means more value per academy and a higher
  plan.

**Wait on:**

- Spark-only or very new academies with no written Contract yet. They'll get
  value later but they won't feel it in a 30-day pilot.
- Academies with a strict "no screens / no AI" stance. Don't argue with them;
  add them to the newsletter and let the network do the work.

## Where they are

| Channel | Who's there | How we show up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Acton owner community (forums, group chats, the network's shared channels) | Owners | Share a real story (an anonymized contradiction the scan caught), not an ad. Offer the free scan. |
| Acton gatherings, owner meetups, regional events | Owners, Guides | Learner-run demo table. Scan sign-up on a QR code. |
| Owner-to-owner referrals | Owners | A referral credit (see [03](03-pricing-and-costs.md#referral-credit)). |
| Learner Exhibitions (ours and others') | Parents, visiting owners | Eagle learners present Eagle Bot or a Tac-On they built as their Exhibition piece. |
| The Tac-On market itself | Learners and admins at academies already on Eagle Bot | Author credit on every Tac-On, "built at Eagle Academy". |
| Parent newsletters of pilot academies | Parents | A one-page "how your studio uses AI" explainer we write for them. |

## The one-sentence version for each person

- **Owner:** "Your studio's Contract, Town Halls and elections in one place,
  run by learners, with AI that follows the studio's rules instead of making
  its own."
- **Guide:** "Stop being the Contract's memory. The learners can look it up."
- **Learner:** "Your studio's government, in an app you can add to yourself,
  and other academies can use what you build."
- **Parent:** "The AI only organizes what the learners already wrote and voted
  on. It doesn't grade, decide, or talk to your kid."
