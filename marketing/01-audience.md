# 01 · Audience

Every Acton academy is its own small business. The network sorts academies into
zones and has its own IT department, but it doesn't buy software for them. The
owner (sometimes with a co-owner or head Guide) can adopt a tool on their own
authority, and they decide quickly if they see it working. Background on the
network is in
[Acton_Context/acton-network.md](../Acton_Context/acton-network.md).

## Who's involved in a sale

| Role | What they care about | What they need from us | Their part in the sale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Enrollment, reputation with parents, staying true to Acton | Proof it puts learners more in charge of their studio, not less | **Buyer.** Signs up and pays. |
| **Guide** | Learners who can settle "what does the Contract actually say?" themselves | Something the learners set up and run without them | **Champion or blocker.** If a Guide hates it, the pilot dies. |
| **Learner (Hero / Eagle)** | Owning their studio, real responsibility, building things people use | A tool for organizing *their* rules, which they set up and run | **Daily user and the reason it exists.** |
| **Parent** | Safety, privacy, "is my kid learning?" | A clear answer about data and AI | **Veto risk.** Rarely involved, but one worried parent can stop a rollout. |

**What Eagle Bot is for.** Learners write and vote on their own Contract, and
they're the ones who have to keep track of it. No academy has Guides or owners
memorizing every rule, note and position. Eagle Bot helps the **learners**
organize their own rules. Pitch it that way to every role.

## Journey Tracker is already there

Most academies use **Journey Tracker**, the network's own software, for goals,
points, badges, Quest progress and Hero/Eagle Bucks. Eagle Bot doesn't replace
any of it and shouldn't be pitched against it. Journey Tracker covers each
learner's own journey. Eagle Bot covers the studio's self-government: the
Contract, Town Halls, elections and positions. Journey Tracker has none of
those as far as public information shows.

## Who to sell to first

Not every academy is a good first customer. Start with the ones most likely to
say yes and most likely to tell other owners.

**Best early customers:**

- Have **Elementary, Middle Studio or Launchpad** running. Older learners run
  Town Halls well and they're the ones who'll build Tac-Ons. (Some academies
  keep Spark screen-free on purpose; don't pitch Spark.)
- Have a **written Contract and ROE** already (Google Docs count). The
  Contract → Wiki scan needs something to read.
- Have learners who **already complain about finding the current rule** in a
  pile of docs. That's the problem Eagle Bot solves, in their own words.
- Are in a **zone we have a connection to**, or have an owner or Guide who's
  active on LinkedIn or in zone channels. They'll talk about it.
- Have **2+ studios**. More studios means more value per academy and a higher
  plan.

**Wait on:**

- Spark-only or very new academies with no written Contract yet. They'll get
  value later but they won't feel it in a 30-day pilot.

No academy is known to have a rigid no-screens or no-AI policy for the whole
school. If an owner is nervous about AI, use the objection answers in
[04](04-messaging.md#objections). Don't write them off.

## Where they are

Everything here is online. Nothing depends on traveling to another academy.

| Channel | Who's there | How we show up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **LinkedIn** | Owners, Guides, some learners | Direct messages to owners and Guides; posts with a real story (an anonymized contradiction the scan caught), not an ad. Offer the free scan. |
| Facebook, Instagram, other social | Owners, academies' own pages, parents | Share the same stories; comment where academies post about Town Halls and Contracts. |
| **Zones** | Academies in the same zone | Warm introductions through owners we know in a zone. Ask a paying owner to mention it in their zone's channel. |
| Owner's and Guide's Forums (private) | Owners, Guides | We can't post there ourselves. A paying owner can share their story. |
| Owner-to-owner referrals | Owners | A referral credit (see [03](03-pricing-and-costs.md#referral-credit)). |
| The Tac-On market itself | Learners and admins at academies already on Eagle Bot | Author credit on every Tac-On, "built at Eagle Academy". |
| Parent newsletters of pilot academies | Parents | A one-page "how your studio uses AI" explainer we write for them. |

## The one-sentence version for each person

- **Owner:** "Your learners' Contract, Town Halls and elections in one place
  they organize themselves, with AI that follows the studio's rules instead of
  making its own."
- **Guide:** "When someone asks what the Contract says, the learners can look
  it up themselves, because they keep it."
- **Learner:** "Your studio's rules and government, organized by you, in an app
  you can add to yourself. Other academies can use what you build."
- **Parent:** "The AI only organizes what the learners already wrote and voted
  on. It doesn't grade, decide, or talk to your kid."
