# Eagle Bot — Go-to-Market

*The plan for selling Eagle Bot to other academies, run by one person.*

Eagle Bot started as the operating system for one Acton academy. This folder is
the plan for selling it to others: who we sell to, how they find us, what we
charge, what it costs us, and what we do in the first 90 days.

![The Eagle Bot funnel](illustrations/funnel.svg)

## The funnel in one paragraph

Acton owners and Guides talk to each other constantly, inside their zones and on
LinkedIn and other social platforms, and they trust what other academies show
them more than anything we could say. So we don't start with ads. We start by
offering a free **Contract → Wiki scan**: an owner sends us one studio's
Contract, and within a day they get it back as a clean, searchable wiki with
every contradiction and gap flagged. That's the hook. Owners who like it get a
**30-minute live demo call**, then a **30-day pilot in one studio**, then a
**Founding Academy plan**. Once they're paying, their learners can publish
Tac-Ons to the market, and every Tac-On installed at another academy tells that
academy where it came from. That's how we grow without an ad budget, and
without travel.

## Three things that make this different from selling normal school software

1. **It's the learners' tool, not the adults'.** At an Acton academy the
   learners write and keep their own Contract. Eagle Bot helps them organize
   their own rules. It isn't a tool for adults to enforce them. That's the Acton idea, and
   it's the pitch.
2. **The product respects Acton's rules.** The AI never invents a rule, never
   rewrites learner wording, and never lets a Guide's memo override a vote.
   That line is the whole pitch to an owner who is nervous about AI.
3. **Costs are tiny and mostly fixed.** Two subscriptions plus pay-as-you-go
   Claude API. One paying academy covers the subscriptions. See
   [03-pricing-and-costs.md](03-pricing-and-costs.md).
