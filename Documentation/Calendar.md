# The studio calendar

*For everyone. The adding and attendance parts are for guides and admins.*

A studio's year has a shape: sessions, each with a Quest; breaks; field
trips; Exhibitions; Town Halls. That shape usually lives in a guide's head, a
shared Google Calendar and a printout on the wall, and the three don't agree.

The **Calendar** puts it in one place, next to everything else the studio
runs. Guides and admins keep it. Everyone else reads it.

---

## Opening it

**Calendar** in the sidebar. An admin can also make it the page everyone lands
on: **Settings → Display → Opening page**.

### Whose calendar you're looking at

Across the top, pick one of three:

| | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Studio** | That studio's entries, its [group's](Studio-Groups.md), and the whole academy's. Pick the studio from the list next to it. |
| **Studio group** | Everything for every studio in the group, plus the academy's. |
| **Whole academy** | Everything you're allowed to see, each studio in its own colour. |

Next to it are the guides assigned to what you're looking at (see
[Studio guides](Studio-Guides.md)).

A learner sees their own studio, its group and the academy, the same as
anywhere else in Eagle Bot. A guide can look at any studio they can open.

### Views

**Day**, **Week**, **Month**, **Year** and **Schedule**, switched top right.

- **Month** is the planning view. Multi-day things run across the days as bars;
  sessions sit in a band along the top of each week.
- **Week** and **Day** show the hours, with all-day things in a strip on top
  and a red line at the current time.
- **Year** shows the sessions and breaks as a strip across the year, a list
  of the year's sessions with their Quests and length in weeks, and twelve
  small months below. Each day is tinted by its session, and break days are grey.
- **Schedule** lists every day that has something on it, from the day you're
  on. **Show further ahead** loads more.

**Today**, and the arrows next to it, move through time. Click a day's number to
open that day.

The sidebar (on a wide screen) has a small month to jump around in, the
session you're in right now with its Quest and how many days are left, and
checkboxes to show or hide each kind of entry, Town Halls, election deadlines,
each studio and each [Tac-On](Tac-Ons.md). Under that you can colour entries by
studio or by kind, and start weeks on Sunday or Monday. These choices are
remembered in your browser and only change things for you.

**Shortcuts:** `t` today, `d` `w` `m` `y` `a` for the views, `j`/`k` (or the
arrow keys) next and previous, `/` search, and `c` to create (guides and
admins).

### What's on it

| Kind | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Session** | A run of weeks with a **Quest**. Shown as a band, with the week number: *Session 2 · Wk 3 · Build a business*. |
| **Break** | Days nobody is expected in. Shaded on the calendar, and left out of attendance. |
| **Field trip** | A trip out, with where to meet and what to bring. |
| **Exhibition** | Where learners show their work to real outside adults. |
| **Launch** | A Socratic discussion or a Quest's Spark. |
| **Deadline** | A day something is due. |
| **Event** | Anything else. |

Things from elsewhere in Eagle Bot appear on their own. **Town Halls** show on
the day they're held and **elections** show when voting opens and closes.
Click one to go to it. Change them where they live, not here.

**Search** (top right, or `/`) finds entries by title, details, place or Quest.

**Export what's shown (.ics)**, in the sidebar, downloads the entries you're
looking at for Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook. Families find this
useful for trips and breaks.

## Adding and changing things

*Guides and admins.*

- **Create**, top right, or press `c`.
- **Click a day** in the month view to add something on it, or **drag across
  several days** to add something that spans them. This is the quick way to mark
  a break or a session.
- **Drag down the hours** in the week or day view to add something with a time.

The form asks what kind it is, then:

- **Title**, and for a session, **the Quest** for it.
- **First and last day.** Moving the first day keeps the length. A session
  shows how many weeks it runs.
- **All day**, or a start and end time. Sessions and breaks are always whole
  days.
- **Whose calendar**: a studio, a studio group, or the whole academy. A guide
  can put things on any studio or group they can open. The whole academy's
  calendar is for admins, and for guides who can see every studio.
- **Repeats**: daily, weekly on chosen days, or monthly, every so many days,
  weeks or months, ending never, on a date, or after a number of times.
- **Where**, **Details** (Markdown works), and an optional **colour**.

Click any entry to see it. Guides and admins get **Edit**, **Duplicate** and
**Delete** there.

**Drag** an entry to another day to move it, or to another time in the week or
day view. Multi-day bars keep their place under the pointer, so grabbing a trip by
its last day and dropping it one day on moves it one day.

**Repeating entries** ask how far a change reaches: **this one**, **this and
following**, or **all of them**, the same way most calendar apps do. Moving a
whole series moves its days too. A Monday and Wednesday circle dragged a day
later becomes Tuesday and Thursday.

Every addition, change and removal is written to the activity log.

## Attendance

*Guides and admins: the **Attendance** button next to **Create**.*

A month at a glance, for whatever you're looking at: a studio, a group or the
whole academy.

- **Totals**: the attendance rate so far this month, days missed, how many of
  those were sick days, and how many learners are counted.
- **The month**: each day shows how many were in out of how many were expected,
  shaded green through amber to red. Break days are hatched. Future days
  show absences already recorded as *planned*.
- **By learner**: everyone's expected days, days missed and rate, lowest rate
  first, with a search box.

Nobody takes a register for this. It's worked out from what's already known:

- **Who's expected**: each learner's usual days, minus any break that covers
  their studio.
- **Who wasn't in**: absences. Learners record their own under **Settings → My
  account → My attendance**, where they also set the days they're normally in.

Click a day to see who was out. A guide or admin can **record an absence** for a
learner there, for example when a parent calls in, or remove one. A guide can
do this for learners in studios they can open. It's recorded in the same place
as the learner's own and written to the activity log.

## What learners see

The whole calendar, every view, read-only. There's no Create button, nothing
to drag, and no Attendance.

In a studio using the **simple view**, learners get a fourth button at the
bottom, **Coming up**: the Quest they're on and which week it is, then the
next few things as big cards with a picture for each kind. Guides in that
studio still get the full calendar.

## Tac-Ons and the calendar

[Tac-Ons](Tac-Ons.md) can work with the calendar in three ways:

- **Put their own records on it.** A Tac-On's dated records show as entries
  next to the guides', with the Tac-On's name on them. They can't be dragged or
  edited on the calendar; they change when the Tac-On's records do. Clicking
  one can open the Tac-On's page. Each person can hide a Tac-On's entries from
  the sidebar.
- **Read it.** A Tac-On can list what's coming up, and the sessions with their
  Quests. Its install screen says so (*Read the studio calendar*), and it only
  ever shows a person what they could already see.
- **React to it.** A Tac-On can do something when a guide adds, changes or
  removes an entry.

A Tac-On can't add to, move or remove anything guides put on the calendar.
Tac-On authors: see the `calendar` section of [TacScript](TacScript.md).

## Setting it up

The calendar requires both `calendar_events` (events and sessions) and
`studio_guides` (the guide assignments shown in the calendar header; see
[Studio guides](Studio-Guides.md)). Attendance also uses the existing `absences`
table and `users.attendance_days` column.

### Verify development first

`shared/schema.ts` declares the schema. `drizzle.config.ts` and `server/db.ts`
use the environment's `DATABASE_URL`. In the workspace, that is the development
database, not the published app's managed production database.

After pulling schema changes, compare the actual development database with the
declarations: all tables and columns, types and lengths, nullability, defaults,
primary and foreign keys (including delete actions), and indexes. For these two
tables, expect:

| Table | Columns | Constraints | Named secondary indexes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `calendar_events` | 19 | Primary key `id`; foreign keys to academy, studio, studio group, and creator | `calendar_events_academy_idx`; `calendar_events_range_idx` on academy and start/end dates |
| `studio_guides` | 7 | Primary key `id`; foreign keys to academy, user, studio, studio group, and assigner | `studio_guides_academy_idx`; `studio_guides_user_idx` |

If development is missing declared changes, preview the existing Drizzle
workflow before applying them:

```sh
npm run db:push -- --strict --verbose
```

Stop if the preview proposes dropping or truncating tables, losing data,
renaming objects ambiguously, or other unexpected changes. Do not use `--force`
to bypass warnings. For reviewed, safe additive changes, apply with
`npm run db:push -- --verbose` in **development only**. Independently inspect the
result afterward: a confirmation prompt can exit without applying changes in a
non-interactive shell, and a successful build is not schema verification.

Sign in through the normal development login and check `/calendar` and a
calendar-dependent Tac-On page (`/t/<install-id>/<page>`). Check their API
responses too: `/api/calendar?from=YYYY-MM-DD&to=YYYY-MM-DD` and
`/api/tacons/view/<install-id>/<page>`. Verify academy and studio boundaries with
authorized accounts; do not disable authentication or permissions for testing.

### Synchronize the published app without replacing its data

For Replit's **managed production database**, schema synchronization happens in
the supported **Publish / Republish** flow, which compares the actual
development and production schemas. Updating the TypeScript declarations or
running `db:push` in the workspace alone does not update production.

1. Obtain explicit approval from the app owner before publishing. The owner
   must initiate **Publish / Republish** in Replit's Publishing pane.
2. Keep the existing managed production database selected. Leave **“Set up
   your production database with your current development data”** (or any
   overwrite/reset/copy-development-data option) **off**. Replacing production
   data would replace real academy users, records, and Tac-On installations.
3. Review the schema changes. If only these tables are missing, expect creation
   of both tables with their columns, defaults, keys, sequences, and indexes.
   They are new tables, not renames of existing ones. If a rename prompt,
   destructive change, or unexpected change appears, stop and have it reviewed
   before confirming. Do not approve a drop-and-add as a substitute for a rename.
4. Complete publishing and wait for the new build to become healthy.
5. Recheck production metadata read-only, including both required tables and
   the rest of the schema. Confirm existing academy data and installed Tac-Ons
   remain intact. New tables may legitimately be empty; do not seed them or
   copy development rows as part of this repair.
6. Sign in to the published app with an authorized account. Open the originally
   affected **installed** Tac-On page and the ordinary Calendar. Check their
   API responses and recent publishing logs for missing-relation errors,
   especially `calendar_events` and `studio_guides`.

Do not run Drizzle against production, add production migration scripts, put
schema mutation in the deployment build, or add startup-time schema repair.
Do not reinstall or republish Tac-On packages to fix missing base-app tables.

If production already has both tables and matches development, do not push or
republish unnecessarily: test the original page and inspect recent logs first.
Schema verification alone is not proof that the signed-in pages work. If
publishing or an authorized live session is unavailable, report exactly which
owner action and live-page checks are still pending.

References: [Replit production databases](https://docs.replit.com/cloud-services/storage-and-databases/production-databases)
and [development and production databases](https://docs.replit.com/features/data-and-storage/development-and-production).
